Menu Planner

Build the week’s set menu from The Source and set the guest numbers for each day.

  • Drag recipes into lunch and dinner for each day
  • Enter guest numbers and which dietary groups are staying
  • See portions needed per dish
Reads: The Source. Feeds: Allergen Check, Brakes Order.

Allergen Check

Enter each group’s allergy information and Kitchen Master checks it against the week’s menu.

  • Flags every dish that contains or may contain a group’s allergens
  • Warns about recipes whose allergens are still unconfirmed
  • Suggests the safe alternative for each meal
Reads: The Source, Menu Planner.

Stock Take

Count what is on the shelves and compare it against what Kitchen Master expects you to have.

  • Manual count entry that works on a tablet
  • Expected amount from deliveries, usage, waste and production
  • Highlights differences so you can spot losses
Reads: Brakes Order, Waste & Produced, The Source.

Waste & Produced

Log what was thrown away and what was made, so stock stays accurate.

  • Quick waste entry by ingredient or dish
  • Record batches produced and their portions
  • Adjusts the expected stock automatically
Feeds: Stock Take.

Brakes Order

Turns the week’s menu and guest numbers into a food order, minus what you already have in stock.

  • Totals every ingredient by Brakes product code
  • Checks it against expected stock
  • Approve the order, then copy the codes into the Brakes portal
Reads: The Source, Menu Planner, Stock Take.
